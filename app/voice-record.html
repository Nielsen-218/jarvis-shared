Record my Creole voice

For a clean recording
  • A quiet room: no fan, television or music.
  • Hold the phone 15 to 20 cm from your mouth, a little to the side.
  • Read at your natural pace, the way you speak at home.
  • One sentence a take. Press, read, press again.
  • If a sentence is not written the way you would say it, tap Edit and fix it first.
